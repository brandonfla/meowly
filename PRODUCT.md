# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (plus minimal vanilla JS for progressive enhancement only). No build step, no server, no framework. The site must work by opening `index.html` or from any static host.

## Users

Everyone: anyone who wants a cat to warm up to them, whether owners, guests meeting a friend's cat, or cat-café visitors. Tone is playful and light.

## Product Purpose

Meowly is a friendly guide to how to make a cat like you: practical, behaviour-based tips (slow blinks, letting the cat approach, reading body language, play, treats, respecting space) told with charm. Success is a visitor who leaves knowing a few concrete things to try at their next cat encounter.

## Positioning

Playful, gentle, non-preachy cat etiquette, grounded in commonly accepted cat-behaviour advice rather than gimmicks.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single static page (sections for the tips), no forms, no data collection, no external services required to function.
- Visual brief from the user (binding): colours pink, purple and blue; the visitor should feel like they are up in the clouds at sunset.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Meowly**.
- Voice: playful, warm, light-hearted.

## Evidence on Hand

- No user-supplied photos or copy. Imagery is a combination of drawn/SVG illustration and free-licensed cat photographs.
- No testimonials, statistics, experts or studies to cite; do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Let the cat choose: every tip centres the cat's consent and comfort.
2. Concrete over cute: each tip is something a visitor can actually do.
3. Charm without clutter: playful voice, calm page.
